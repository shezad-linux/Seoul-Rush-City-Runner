This policy explains how Seoul Rush: City Runner handles game progress, ads, and privacy choices. The game can be played without creating an account.

Game data stored on your device

The game stores progress and settings locally on your device. This can include scores, coins, unlocked items, tutorial progress, sound preferences, and ad reward or frequency records. The game does not require a player account.

Uninstalling the app may remove this local data. Android backup behavior may depend on your device and Google account settings.

Advertising and related data

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including Unity Ads mediation, to show ads. Ad formats may include rewarded, interstitial, banner, native, rewarded interstitial, and app open ads. Ads may be used to support the game or offer optional in-game rewards.

These ad providers may process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, device and app information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Their handling of this data depends on your region, device settings, consent choices, and their policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Unity’s Privacy Policy.

The app checks whether consent information or a privacy form is required before requesting ads. Where required, the form presents available choices. The Settings screen displays a Privacy Options control when the consent system requires it.

Your choices

  • Use the in-app Privacy Options control when it appears in Settings to review available advertising choices.
  • Use your Android device’s privacy settings to manage advertising identifiers and related controls.
  • Choose whether to watch an optional rewarded ad. Other ad placements may still appear during play.
  • Uninstall the game to remove locally stored progress, subject to your device’s backup settings.

Sharing and retention

Local game progress is stored on your device. Advertising providers receive and process data when their services operate, as described above. Their retention periods and deletion procedures are governed by their own policies. We do not sell your locally saved game progress.

Children

The game’s intended audience and any age-specific treatment must be reflected in the final store listing and ad configuration. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, use the contact method below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the game, its ad services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact details on the game’s Google Play listing.
